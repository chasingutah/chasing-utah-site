02 / Build in public

Less hype. More field notes.

What did we try? What actually happened? What broke? Who helped? What would we change next time?

The goal is a living journal of repeatable experiments: a two-node mesh test, an antenna build, a home resilience walk-through, a community interview, or a prototype shipped in a weekend.

See the public site code ↗
Field-note template

Question: What are we testing?

Method: What equipment, conditions and steps?

Evidence: What can others verify?

Outcome: What worked, failed or remains unknown?

Next: What will we change?

Published reports, datasets and videos will be linked here as they become ready. None are implied by this template.

05 / A simple operating code

Receipts before prophecy. Dignity before scale.

We can dream a hundred thousand years forward and still start with a useful test this Saturday. Wild imagination and disciplined evidence belong at the same table.

Open knowledge when possible. Permission before publishing people or their work. Credit collaborators. Disclose money and affiliations. Share failures. Protect private IP. Choose work that makes a real difference.
